#3: Naruto

Naruto is an all-time classic that taught me about perseverance and friendship. Watching Naruto Uzumaki go from an outcast to Hokage is one of the most rewarding stories in anime. The ninja world is full of creative jutsu, emotional flashbacks, and unforgettable rivalries that make it a must-watch.

  • Created by Masashi Kishimoto
  • Follows Naruto's dream to become Hokage
  • Includes both Naruto and Naruto: Shippuden series
