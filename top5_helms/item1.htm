#1: Chainsaw Man

Chainsaw Man is my number one favorite anime because of its wild and unpredictable story. The mix of intense action, dark humor, and emotional moments makes every episode exciting. Denji's journey from a poor devil hunter to Chainsaw Man is both thrilling and heartbreaking, and the animation by MAPPA is absolutely stunning.

  • Created by Tatsuki Fujimoto
  • Animated by Studio MAPPA
  • Features unique devil powers and creative fight scenes
