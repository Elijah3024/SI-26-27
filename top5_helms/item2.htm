#2: Bleach

Bleach holds a special place as my second favorite anime with its epic battles and deep lore. Ichigo Kurosaki's growth as a Soul Reaper is inspiring, and the Thousand-Year Blood War arc brings the series to new heights. The stylish character designs and intense sword fights keep me hooked every time I rewatch it.

  • Created by Tite Kubo
  • Features Soul Reapers, Hollows, and the Soul Society
  • Known for its unique bankai transformations
