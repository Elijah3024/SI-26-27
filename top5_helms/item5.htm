#5: Jujutsu Kaisen

Jujutsu Kaisen rounds out my top 5 with its perfect blend of horror, action, and heart. Yuji Itadori's fight against curses while hosting Sukuna is intense and full of twists. The animation quality is top-tier, the characters are complex, and the battles are some of the most creative in modern anime.

  • Created by Gege Akutami
  • Animated by Studio MAPPA
  • Features cursed techniques and domain expansions
