#4: Dragon Ball

Dragon Ball is the foundation of modern shonen anime and one of my all-time favorites. Goku's endless adventures, from collecting Dragon Balls to fighting powerful villains, never get old. The action is legendary, the humor is great, and the series continues to inspire new generations of fans with its positive messages about hard work and never giving up.

  • Created by Akira Toriyama
  • Includes Dragon Ball, Dragon Ball Z, Super, and more
  • Famous for Super Saiyan transformations and epic battles
